# Princess Room X

See what you really earn on TikTok Shop — per product, per video, per day.

Drop the order export TikTok gives you and the page works out what was actually
paid: refunded and cancelled orders left out, organic and ads told apart by the
commission plan, every product under its own listing.

Everything happens inside your own browser. The file never leaves your phone,
there is no account and no server: the figures are saved in the browser's own
storage and are still there next time you open it.

Open it here: https://celialon.github.io/princess-room-x/
